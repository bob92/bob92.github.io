Common questions

How do I add a feed?
Open Edit Feeds and tap Add Feed. Pick from the built-in library, search for a site, or paste a website or feed address.

Can I bring my feeds from another reader?
Yes. Export an OPML file from your old reader, then import it in Mango from Settings › Data and Sync.

Do my feeds sync between my devices?
Yes, through your own iCloud account, when you're signed in to iCloud on each device. There's no Mango account.

I bought Mango Pro on another device. How do I get it back?
Open Get Mango Pro in Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Why do some articles open as a web page?
Mango shows most articles in its own reader. When a site's page can't be shown that way, Mango opens the original page instead.
