Privacy Policy

Mango · Effective October 22, 2026

The short version: Mango has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your feeds, bookmarks and reading history stay on your devices and in your own iCloud account. We, the makers of Mango, never receive them.

What Mango stores

To work, Mango keeps the following on your device:

  • the feeds you subscribe to and how you've organised them into pages,
  • articles and images it has downloaded, so they open quickly and work offline,
  • which articles you've read, and the articles you've bookmarked,
  • your settings, themes and filters.

The Mango widget and the Share extension use the same data on your device.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, Mango syncs your feeds, read status, bookmarks, settings and Mango Pro status between your devices, using Apple's iCloud services under your own Apple Account. This data is stored by Apple and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We can't see or access it. You can turn iCloud off for Mango at any time in your device's Settings.

Connecting to websites

Mango is a reader: it connects directly to the websites whose feeds you follow to download their feeds, articles and images. Like any web browser, these requests reveal your IP address to those websites, and they're subject to each site's own privacy policy.

A few other services are contacted to show content:

  • Website icons. To show a feed's icon, Mango may ask DuckDuckGo's and Google's icon services for the icon of that site's domain (for example, “nytimes.com”). Only the domain is sent.
  • Embedded media. When an article contains a video, podcast, song or other embed (for example YouTube, Vimeo, Spotify or SoundCloud), Mango loads it from that service, which receives the embed's address and may apply its own privacy policy and cookies.
  • Web pages. When you open an article as a web page or in the in-app browser, the page loads directly from the publisher's site.

Purchases

Mango Pro is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Mango never sees your payment details; it only receives confirmation from Apple that the purchase was made.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal information or create accounts.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone.

Contacting us

If you email us for support, we'll use your email address and whatever you choose to send only to reply to you. We don't add it to any mailing list.

Children

Mango doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting Mango removes its data from your device. To remove what's synced, also delete Mango's data from iCloud in your device's Settings (your name › iCloud › Storage). You can also erase your feeds, bookmarks and settings with Reset App in Settings › Data and Sync.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
